About

Short bio

Bahman Rostami-Tabar is a Professor of Analytics and Decision Sciences in the Department of Logistics and Operations Management at Cardiff Business School, Cardiff University. He is the founder and Director of the Data Lab for Social Good Research Group at Cardiff University and the founder of the Forecasting for Social Good community at the International Institute of Forecasters. He also leads the Research Excellence Framework (2029) Unit of Assessment 17 (Business and Management Studies) at Cardiff University.

Bahman’s research lies at the intersection of uncertainty & future, analytics and decision sciences, with a particular emphasis on probabilistic modelling, forecasting, and operational research for planning and decision-making under uncertainty. His work is strongly motivated by real-world impact and has contributed to sectors focused on social good, including healthcare operations, global health and humanitarian logistics and supply chains, agriculture and food systems, social sustainability, and governmental policy.

He is the author of over 50 research papers and one book. In recognition of his contributions to Operational Research and decision sciences, Bahman received the Goodeve Medal in 2024 from the Operational Research Society for outstanding contributions to the field. In 2025, he was awarded the Lyn Thomas Impact Medal for academic OR research demonstrating both methodological novelty and clear, evidenced real-world impact.

Background

I am Kurdish, born and raised in Kurdistan in the village of Bayangan, Paveh, Kermanshah, in western Iran. Just a short half-hour drive from the border of Iraqi Kurdistan, this land of mountains, resilience, and deep history shaped who I am.

After high school, I moved to Tehran and studied Industrial Engineering, receiving my BSc from K.N. Toosi University of Technology in 2004. Following my graduation, I worked in various companies in different industries as a planning engineer in Tehran and the south of Iran. I then decided to continue my studies and received my MSc in Industrial Engineering from Tarbiat Modares University in 2008. After working in the project management field for two years, I moved to France in 2010 to pursue a PhD, and received my PhD from the University of Bordeaux in 2014. Following that, I spent two years at Ecole Centrale Paris (now CentraleSupélec) for my postdoctoral research.
