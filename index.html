Research focus

Uncertainty

The future is not known with certainty, and a single number hides this. I quantify uncertainty and communicate it in a form that decision-makers can use.

Analytics

I develop and apply methods from Data Science, AI, and Operations Research that are methodologically rigorous and relevant to real-world decision contexts.

Decision quality

A forecast or a model has value only if it leads to a better decision. I evaluate analytical methods by the quality of the decisions they support, not by accuracy alone.
