Professor Bahman Rostami-Tabar

Uncertainty & Futures→Analytics→Decision quality
Professor of Analytics and Decision Sciences, Cardiff University

My work sits at the intersection of data, uncertainty, and decision-making. Drawing on Data Science, AI, and Operations Research, I develop and apply methods that support better choices in complex and uncertain futures.

Research focus

Uncertainty & Futures

The future is uncertain, and a single number rarely tells the whole story. I make uncertainty visible by combining qualitative insights about what cannot be measured with quantitative evidence about what can, and translating both into information that supports better decisions.

Analytics

I develop and apply methods from Data Science, AI, Forecasting and Operations Research. They are methodologically rigorous and relevant to real-world decision contexts, and I work on them closely with academic, public, and industry partners who use them in practice.

Decision quality

A model has value only if it leads to a better decision. I evaluate analytical methods by the quality of the decisions they support, not by accuracy alone, and I examine how forecasts and uncertainty information are used in planning and policy.
